# Case: Limitations Reflected In Weak Recommendation Should Not Be Overpenalized

Input: A guideline has limitations in evidence synthesis but explicitly
describes them in EtD and connects them to weak, conditional, no recommendation,
or limited practice wording.

Expected: Record the limitation and assess traceability, but do not
mechanically downgrade whole-CPG trustworthiness solely because the evidence is
limited or the recommendation is weak/conditional.
