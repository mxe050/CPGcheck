# Regression case: qualitative SR with transparent certainty should not be overpenalized

Synthetic input:
A guideline does not pool results because populations, interventions, outcomes,
and follow-up differ. It reports study-level effects, direction, uncertainty,
RoB, indirectness, inconsistency and imprecision rationale, and reflects
uncertainty in EtD and conditional recommendation wording.

Expected behavior:
Do not downgrade merely because meta-analysis is absent or qualitative synthesis
is used. A mandatory alert is unnecessary or minor if the certainty basis is
transparent.
