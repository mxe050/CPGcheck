# Regression case: formal SR claim is not substantive validity in domestic scan

Synthetic input:
A domestic guideline states that SR was performed and reports databases, search
period, study counts, meta-analysis, forest plots, and effect estimates. The
materials do not show primary-study independence, duplicate-review overlap,
design-specific RoB, observational confounding, outcome-specific certainty, or
EtD linkage.

Expected behavior:
The audit must separate "Formal SR claim" from "Substantive SR validity." It
must not conclude only "SR claim supported." The Final conclusion must report
whether SR conceptual failure is present, possible, not found, or not
assessable.
