# mode_japanese_cpg_full_article_and_annotated_references

Input scenario: The user selects 【日本のCPG問題重点】 for a Japanese domestic CPG.

Expected behavior:
- Output includes the Japanese domestic 12-row inspection table.
- Applicable or suspected rows cite guideline locations and short wording or
  faithful summaries.
- Each applicable or suspected row explains why the guideline wording fits that
  12-item candidate pattern.
- Output includes Final CPG trustworthiness conclusion.
- Output continues after Final with a short summary, the full 12-item
  educational article, and an annotated reference list.
- References include brief explanations of methodological relevance.

Forbidden behavior:
- Do not omit the 12-item educational article.
- Do not provide only a table and Final.
- Do not list references without explaining why they matter.
- Do not convert the 12-row inspection table into a rating score.
