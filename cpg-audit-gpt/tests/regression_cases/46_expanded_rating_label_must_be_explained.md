# Expanded rating label must be explained

Scenario: A guideline has an A-level evidence-to-recommendation chain for most
major recommendations, but one linked COI management document is referenced
rather than visible and one evidence profile has a minor correction alert that
does not affect direction, strength, certainty, or EtD.

Expected: The final conclusion may use "A with mandatory audit alerts" or
"A-minus" if justified, but it must not output the expanded label alone. It must
state the nearest ordinary rating family, why the modifier is needed, which
alerts remain, why those alerts are or are not rating-lowering defects, and what
confirmation would move the rating upward or what unresolved finding would move
it downward.

Forbidden: "Final CPG rating candidate: A-minus" with no explanation of how the
label relates to the ordinary rating scale or what the remaining alerts mean.
