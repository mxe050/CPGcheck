# Case: SR Claim Formal Support Is Not Substantive Validity

Input: A guideline reports search databases, included studies, meta-analysis,
forest plots, and effect estimates.

Expected: Treat these as formal SR claim support only. Substantive SR validity
requires PICO, primary-study-unit independence, design-specific RoB,
confounding assessment, outcome-specific certainty, SoF/Evidence Profile, and
EtD connection.
