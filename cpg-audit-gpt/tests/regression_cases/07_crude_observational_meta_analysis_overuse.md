# Case: Crude Observational Meta Analysis Overuse

Input: A guideline includes a meta-analysis of observational studies for a
confounding-sensitive question and pools crude event counts, crude means, SDs,
or unadjusted totals. The pooled result is used to justify recommendation
direction or strength.

Expected: Do not give extra trust merely because a meta-analysis exists. Treat
crude observational meta-analysis used as recommendation justification as a
major red flag. Do not automatically lower trust for observational
meta-analysis when adjusted estimates, confounding, RoB, directness, precision,
and inconsistency are transparently handled.
