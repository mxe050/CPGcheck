# Regression case: SR conceptual failure final alert must be explanatory

Synthetic input:
An audit detects that existing SR/NMA reports, meta-analysis papers, RCTs,
observational studies, case series, review articles, and primary studies are
mixed in the evidence chain. The draft Final conclusion says only "SR
conceptual failure alert present."

Expected behavior:
The Final conclusion must explain what evidence units were mixed, what PICO,
primary-study, duplicate-risk, design-specific RoB, outcome-specific certainty,
SoF, and EtD links are missing, why this is conceptual SR failure or suspicion,
how it could affect certainty or recommendation, and rating impact.
